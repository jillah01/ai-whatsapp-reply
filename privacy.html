Privacy Policy

ReplyAI uses Supabase for account authentication and storage of business profile and reply history data. Reply generation is performed through a server-side Cloudflare Worker using an AI provider.

Messages entered to generate a reply are sent to the AI service for that purpose. Do not enter passwords, payment card data, or highly sensitive personal information into customer messages.

Account information and saved replies are used to provide the service. We do not publish your private account data.

This policy describes the current MVP and may be updated as the service changes.

Contact: add your support email before public launch.
